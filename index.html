The Céladon Philosophy

Crafted for Daily Rituals, Built to Last Generations.

Founded in 2018 in an open-air stone barn, Céladon Atelier crafts tableware inspired by Japanese wabi-sabi balance and modern Scandinavian simplicity. We reject chemical factory coatings in favor of food-safe feldspar and natural wood ash glazes.

Responsibly Sourced Mineral Clay

Harvested with minimal environmental footprint and 100% recycled greywater kiln systems.

Individually Wheel-Thrown & Hand-Finished

Every plate and mug bears the subtle organic thumbprint of its maker, making each item unique.

Reinforced Commercial Durability

Vitrified at extreme temperatures to prevent edge chipping, thermal shock, and glaze crazing.
